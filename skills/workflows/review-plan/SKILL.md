name: review-plan
description:
  Review a proposed or revised work plan for clarity, feasibility, missing decisions, and agreement with project
  constraints. Use for requested plan review, not implementation review or a whole-project audit.
---

# Plan review

Read the requested plan, applicable charters and decisions, related records, and relevant source. Follow the project's
chosen planning method without imposing a different framework. Separate future work from delivered results, and
confirmed human decisions from assumptions or proposals.

Check whether another agent could understand the intended outcome and execute the next work without the originating
conversation. Investigate contradictions, missing acceptance criteria, dependencies, failure and recovery behavior,
verification gaps, and unjustified scope. Check that newly discovered work and revised answers have been reconciled
throughout the plan. Assess the parts material to this work; do not fill a checklist with speculative concerns. Check
that the human overview explains why, what, and how with useful prose and visuals, while implementing-agent details
remain clearly separate and proportionate.

For multi-task work, check dependency readiness, preferred order, shared changes that constrain parallel execution, and
the authorized execution scope. Check acceptance criteria and how verified implementation will be presented for any
reserved developer completion acceptance; planning approval and permission to publish remain distinct.

Check that major-feature implementation has a clear human approval gate covering the current plan and material scope
changes. Distinguish that gate from authorized small audit fixes and refinement of work already underway. Identify
unresolved decisions that prevent approval; never infer approval from a status field, review verdict, or passing checks.

When fresh-context review is available and authorized, supply the reviewer with the requested scope, plan, relevant
authority, and source references, without coaching toward the author's preferred verdict or importing the conversation.
Choose perspectives proportionate to the risks. Respect delegation restrictions; otherwise perform a same-context review
and state that limitation. Planning can continue without claiming an independent review occurred.

Return prioritized findings with a concrete location, evidence, consequence, and recommended resolution. Distinguish
blockers from improvements and open questions; say when no material issue is established. Preserve a review report where
requested. Do not rewrite agreed requirements, invent human answers, or change implementation during review. If
revisions are requested, update the existing plan and its material questions and answers, preserving the reasons for
changed decisions; honor authority already granted.

Bound review effort and stop when actionable findings are resolved, explicitly deferred, or require a developer's
decision. Report readiness and evidence limitations without declaring a review equivalent to approval or successful
implementation. Do not stage or commit unless requested.
