# ADR-0: Connected records

Keep decisions connected to the evidence that supports them.

Explore the [example experiment](../experiments/EXP-00.md), its [environment](../environments/ENV-00.md), and the
[supporting citation](../citations/CIT-00.json). Hover or focus these links to preview their records.

Rationale: Connected records make the origin and consequences of a decision easier to inspect.
