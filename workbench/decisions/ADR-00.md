# ADR-0: Improving Workbench through use

While using Workbench in any project, agents propose fixes, additions, and improvements to its tools, record
conventions, guidance, and examples. Present the observed problem or opportunity, supporting evidence, a concrete
proposal, expected benefits, and material tradeoffs to the developer.

For apparent gaps between intended and observed behavior, explain the documented or inferred intent and state
uncertainties about intent or cause. Account for project-specific adaptations when identifying reusable changes to
Workbench's shared material.

Rationale: Use in diverse projects reveals unmet needs and opportunities that Workbench's own examples and reviews
cannot anticipate. Concrete proposals let developers turn that experience into improvements shared across projects.
