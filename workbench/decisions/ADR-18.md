# ADR-18: Section 508 conformance

Projects adopting this decision require released information and communication technology to conform to the applicable
technical requirements of the [Revised Section 508 Standards](https://www.access-board.gov/ict/). This is a project
quality requirement independent of whether the project has a statutory Section 508 obligation.

The requirement covers software interfaces, electronic content, and documentation and support within the project's
control, including complete user workflows. Applicable requirements include the incorporated WCAG criteria and the
additional software, assistive technology interoperability, and support requirements; WCAG conformance alone does not
establish Section 508 conformance.

Conformance evidence identifies the evaluated release, supported environments, applicable requirements, assessment
methods, results, and known limitations. Inapplicability and any permitted equivalent facilitation have explicit
justification. Evidence includes human assessment where automated checks cannot establish the required behavior. Claims
remain bounded by that evidence; unresolved failures of applicable requirements preclude a full conformance claim.

Rationale: A shared accessibility standard makes intended access assessable across interfaces and documents. Explicit
scope and evidence keep conformance claims meaningful as products and their supported environments change.
